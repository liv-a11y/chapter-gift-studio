# First PlaceUX

I want to build sample UX for my investor deck for Good Chapter.

Scenario: Maya Ellison, 34, buying a house alone (First Place chapter).
Brands to feature: Otherland, Framebridge, Ghia, Caraway.
Format: High-fidelity sample UX screens ready for presentation and investor deck slides.

Core mechanics to showcase:
1. Starter Flow (Friend): Picking the "First Place" chapter, selecting 8 curated items across the four price bands ($25–50 Easy yes, $60–120 Considered, $40–150 Practical, $200–400 Group candidate) with one reason each, and adding Maya's guest circle.
2. Recipient Private Review (Maya): The quiet, private approval step. Maya reviews the 8 items, keeps or cuts privately (friend never sees edits), enters delivery details, and approves in two clicks.
3. Guest Giving Page: Sent from the friend (never Maya), displaying the 8 items with their one-line reasons, guest circle notes, and a fast 5-minute giving flow.
